Kandams

Explore the Chapters of Your Life

In Nadi astrology, every leaf is divided into Kandams — distinct chapters that each address a different area of your life, from general life overview to career, relationships, health and spiritual growth. Rather than a single broad prediction, a full Nadi reading moves through these chapters one at a time, giving you the option to focus on the areas most relevant to your current questions.

General Life

An overview of your life's path and key turning points.

Wealth & Property

Perspective on finances, assets and property matters.

Children & Family

Understanding family bonds and matters of progeny.

Health

Guidance on wellbeing and health-related concerns.

Marriage & Relationships

Insights on compatibility, timing and married life.

Spiritual Journey

Guidance for those seeking a deeper spiritual path.

Career & Business

Guidance on profession, growth and business decisions.

Dosha & Remedies

Identifying doshas and prescribed traditional remedies.
