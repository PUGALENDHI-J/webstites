Karma Kandam — Career & Profession

Karma Kandam is the career-focused chapter of your Nadi leaf, providing predictions about your professional path, job opportunities and prospects for business growth.

This Kandam frequently addresses potential changes in profession, relocation possibilities tied to work, and the general direction your career is likely to take across different life stages.

  • Career path and professional direction
  • Job opportunities and business growth prospects
  • Guidance on career changes or relocation
  • Favourable periods for professional decisions

What Karma Kandam Reveals

Whether you're an employee weighing a job change, an entrepreneur considering a new venture, or someone facing uncertainty about your professional direction, this Kandam is designed to offer clarity grounded in the specific details recorded on your leaf.

It's often read alongside Kudumba Kandam (wealth and education) for a fuller financial and professional picture, since career decisions and financial stability are closely intertwined in Nadi tradition.
