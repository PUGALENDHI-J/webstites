About ABASS

A trust dedicated to devotion and community wellbeing

ABASS works across religious, educational, cultural and welfare-oriented activities, as set out in the objects of its Trust Deed.

Religious & Spiritual Activities

Supporting temple activities and Ayyappa Seva, and advancing Indian religious culture, ethics and moral values.

Education

Establishing educational institutions, hostels and Gurukulams, and supporting scholarships and student assistance.

Culture & Heritage

Encouraging art, music, dance and cultural programmes that preserve and celebrate community heritage.

Social Welfare

Extending relief, rehabilitation and support to the poor, the elderly, orphans and those in distress.
