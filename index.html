Heritage & Foundation

A legacy of faith, service and community

Rooted in Zamin Pallavaram, Chennai, the Trust continues an unbroken tradition of Ayyappa Seva, community Annadhaanam and support for devotees.

80-Year Heritage

Serving the Ayyappa devotee community continuously since 1946 across generations with steadfast devotion.

Registered Public Trust

Formally constituted as a registered public charitable trust with transparent governance and structured administration.

Annadhaanam & Seva

Regular Annadhaanam and seva for thousands of devotees during Mandalam, Makaravilakku and special pooja occasions.

Spiritual & Cultural

Preserving traditional worship practices, Vilakku Pooja, Padi Pooja, devotional music and spiritual heritage.

Objects of the Trust

Purposes defined in the Trust Deed

The Trust operates exclusively for public charitable and religious purposes, advancing spiritual, social and community welfare.

01

Religious & Spiritual Advancement

Conducting daily and special poojas, Padi Pooja, Vilakku Pooja, bhajans and spiritual discourses in honour of Lord Ayyappa.

02

Annadhaanam & Food Relief

Providing free food (Annadhaanam) to devotees, pilgrims, underprivileged families and the local community during religious festivals.

03

Pilgrim Assistance & Guidance

Supporting Sabarimala pilgrims with Kettu Nira facilities, travel guidance, resting amenities and spiritual orientation.

04

Community Welfare & Education

Supporting educational assistance, medical camps, relief activities and cultural enrichment programs for the society.
