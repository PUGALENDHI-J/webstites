Vaitheeswaran Koil Home of Nadi Jothidam

Ancient Wisdom. Personal Guidance.

Experience Authentic Nadi Jothidam

Nadi Astrology is a traditional system of prediction, associated with ancient palm-leaf manuscripts inscribed centuries ago by Siddhars — enlightened sages believed to have recorded the destinies of individuals yet to be born, preserved near the revered Vaitheeswaran Koil temple in Tamil Nadu.

At Shiva Nadi Jyotish in Bhopal, we help you find the exact leaf bearing your name through your thumb impression, and guide you through what it reveals — with honesty, patience and respect for the tradition.
