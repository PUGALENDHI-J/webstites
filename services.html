Pariharams

What Are Nadi Astrology Remedies?

Nadi remedies, or pariharams, are the spiritual practices prescribed within your leaf reading to ease the effects of past-life karma and correct doshas (afflictions) it reveals. They are not generic suggestions — each one is specific to what your own leaf describes.

Remedies may include temple rituals, mantra japam, charitable acts or ancestral blessing ceremonies, chosen according to the exact issue your reading uncovers, whether that concerns health, relationships, career or family matters.

Types of Remedies

Nadi Remedies We Guide You Through

Temple Visits & Rituals

Specific poojas and parikarams at temples suited to what your leaf identifies, including centres linked to the Navagraha.

Mantra Japam & Homas

Personalised mantra chanting and fire rituals prescribed to invoke blessings and ease specific karmic blocks.

Charitable Acts & Donations

Acts of dharma such as feeding the needy, offering clothes or supporting education, as guided by your leaf.

Ancestral Blessing Rituals

Poojas to honour ancestors and ease Pitru Dosha, addressing patterns linked to family and lineage.

The Difference

How Nadi Remedies Differ From General Astrology Solutions

General Astrology Remedies

  • Often rely on broad interpretations, without a deep personal connection to your record
  • Focus on a single issue rather than your complete life picture
  • May not clearly trace the past-life root cause of a recurring problem

Nadi Remedies & Pariharams

  • Derived directly from your own leaf, preserved through generations of tradition
  • Personalised to your individual karmic pattern, not a one-size-fits-all fix
  • Addresses past, present and future together, with clear, guided steps
